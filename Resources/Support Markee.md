# Thanks for using Markee ❤️

Markee is free and open source, and it always will be. Every feature works
whether you pay or not — there is no catch coming.

## If it's earned a coffee

**[Become a supporter →](https://markee.sbang.dev/support)** — pay what you
want, from $1.99, one time.

Your purchase email includes a license key. Enter it via
**Markee ▸ Enter License Key…** and this note, along with the small heart in
the titlebar, will never appear again. One key covers up to 3 Macs; you can
free up a slot any time from the Polar customer portal.

Supporting pays for the Apple Developer membership that keeps Markee signed
and notarized, and buys time for new features.

## Prefer not to?

Totally fine. Close this window and keep using Markee forever. This note
shows at most once a month, and nothing else changes.
